Find your water meter

You don't need to know the radio ID. Enter the approximate reading displayed on your water meter in m³, then select a matching result. All nearby compatible meters are scanned, even if they aren't assigned.

Assign a meter

A blank key uses the factory PRIOS keys for new assignments, and preserves any previously stored custom key for the same meter. This firmware does not decode AES-128 encrypted OMS frames. Custom keys are stored locally; they are never returned by the web API.

Assigned meters

Each slot appears as fixed ESPHome entities in Home Assistant. If you replace a meter in a slot, its entity history may refer to two different meters; avoid reusing slots unnecessarily.

Alarm flags and flow

Alarm Details shows all decoded status flags, including SENSOR_FRAUD, MECHANICAL_FRAUD, BACK_FLOW, UNDERFLOW and OVERFLOW; stale readings never report OK. Leak Alarm comes directly from the telegram. Flow (estimated) in L/h is calculated from successive total readings; the sample SAP/00/07 telegram does not transmit instantaneous flow.

Connection and safety

The gateway receives only T1 PRIOS frames that pass the radio CRC and PRIOS check byte. A meter with an unknown key or unsupported format is listed as “not decoded”, never as 0 m³. Protect access to this page using a unique web password and your Wi-Fi network. For the ESPHome device page and OTA, visit the built-in ESPHome interface.
